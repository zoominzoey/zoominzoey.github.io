Hi, I'm Zoey Carter

As an 8th generation Pekinite, I have deep roots in Pekin, Illinois. Having grown up, worked, and built my life right here in our community, I have witnessed our successes firsthand as well as the everyday obstacles that working families and local neighborhoods face.

As an everyday citizen, I am deeply passionate about practical infrastructure and how we navigate our city. I understand that properly paved roadways, smart transit flow, and visible signage aren't simply conveniences - they are fundamental to our safety and municipal growth.

Through grassroots involvement, I listen directly to the concerns of my neighbors. I am running for City Council to ensure our local government stays accountable, responsive, and relentlessly focused on the long-term prosperity of Pekin.

Actionable Solutions

My Priorities for Pekin

I am focused on practical, common-sense priorities that directly benefit our neighborhoods and local economy.

Fiscal

Preparing Pekin for long-term fiscal stability means making our municipal operations smarter. I champion modern energy efficiency programs and support initiatives that lower utility burdens for both homeowners and our local municipal budgets.

Modern Municipal Policy

Safe & Accurate Mapping

Every neighborhood deserves safe roads and clear, functional navigation. I am dedicated to addressing chronic pothole issues and ensuring our community mapping is up-to-date so that police, fire, and emergency medical teams can respond swiftly.

Reliable Infrastructure

Grassroots Community Growth

A strong community starts from the ground up. I will work alongside everyday residents to cut unnecessary bureaucratic hurdles, support our neighborhood initiatives, and keep our community resources invested right here in Pekin.
